In this article, a generic method is proposed to effectively enforce flexible confining boundary conditions in SPH. The
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     Onlinemethod makes use of its kernel truncation feature along free edges of SPH domain to automatically locate and trace curvature change of the boundary surfaces. the confining boundaries.
