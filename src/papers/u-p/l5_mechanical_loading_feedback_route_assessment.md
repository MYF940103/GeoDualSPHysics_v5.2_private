# L5 Mechanical Loading And Feedback Route Assessment

Date: 2026-05-14

## Objective

L5 is the future route required for strict mechanical reproduction of the
1D consolidation loading process. This note assesses why L2/L3b failed, what a
paper-faithful route needs, and why this should remain CPU-first.

## Why L2 And L3b Failed The Mechanical Gate

L2 and L3b are numerically stable, but neither reproduces a quasi-static top
surcharge:

- L2 uses `AccInput` on the top `mkfluid=1` material layer. This is a body
  acceleration applied to selected material particles, not a surface traction.
- L3b removes `AccInput` and applies `Fz=q0*A` to detected top material
  particles. This corrects the total force scale but still applies an impulsive
  force directly to material particles rather than through a controlled
  loading plate or traction boundary.
- Both routes create dynamic velocity/divergence response and excess-pressure
  peaks near `6e5 Pa`, while the Terzaghi load scale is `1e4 Pa`.

The failure mode is therefore load generation, not the PR diffusion operator
itself.

## Why AccInput Is Not Surface Surcharge

`AccInput` is a body-force route. Its effect depends on:

- selected material-layer mass;
- layer thickness;
- timestep and dynamic response;
- local velocity/divergence generated by the acceleration impulse.

A surface surcharge is instead a boundary traction or plate load acting on the
top face of the continuum. It should create an undrained pressure increment at
the `q0` scale without launching a strong mechanical wave through the column.

## Why Force-On-Material Is Still Not Enough

The L3b `MechanicalTopLoad` prototype has the right total force:

```text
Fz = q0 * A = -10000 Pa * 0.1 m2 = -1000 N
```

However, distributing that force directly onto material particles still does
not provide a quasi-static surface boundary condition. It is a useful CPU
diagnostic, but not a strict validation route.

## More Paper-Faithful Candidate Routes

### Force-Controlled Loading Plate

A top plate or platen receives the total force `F=q0*A`; the specimen responds
through contact or SPH interaction. This is closer to the paper concept of a
top surcharge, but it requires stable force-control, reaction diagnostics, and
possibly staged equilibration.

### True Surface Traction

A traction boundary applies stress directly on the top surface rather than
material body acceleration. This is conceptually clean but requires careful
source design to define the surface, area weights, and CPU/GPU parity.

### Consistent Total/Effective Stress Initializer

Initialize pore pressure and stress so the total stress state corresponds to
the applied surcharge. This may be the cleanest way to represent the
instantaneous-undrained starting state without a dynamic load impulse, but the
current `InitialStressMode=1` is CPU-only isotropic effective compression and
not a vertical total-stress initializer.

### Quasi-Static Equilibration

Apply loading slowly or in a staged pre-drainage phase, then start the
diffusion comparison. This may be physically defensible only after the surface
load route itself is paper-faithful.

## Should Full Feedback Be Enabled Now?

No. Full feedback should not be enabled as the next step.

Reasons:

- the mechanical load-generation route is not yet correct;
- triaxial work showed that full feedback remains a separate stability
  question;
- enabling feedback while the load route is wrong would mix two blockers;
- L3c already provides a clean feedback-off diffusion/boundary gate.

Full feedback belongs after a CPU mechanical loading route can generate the
correct pressure scale without dynamic over-amplification.

## Required Source Features For L5

Likely L5 source needs:

- default-off CPU surface traction or force-controlled plate support;
- clear target-surface selection and area weighting;
- staged/ramped loading controls;
- diagnostics for applied force, reaction, top velocity, displacement, and
  generated pressure scale;
- optional consistent vertical total-stress initializer;
- GPU hard error until CPU physics is validated.

## Complexity And Risk

L5 is higher risk than L4 because it touches mechanical loading and possibly
feedback coupling. It should be CPU-first and design-first. It should not block
the lower-risk L4 GPU long-run.

## Assessment

L5 is eventually necessary for strict complete 1D reproduction. It is not the
best immediate next step if the project needs a reliable validation figure
quickly. The recommended order is L4 first, then L5.
