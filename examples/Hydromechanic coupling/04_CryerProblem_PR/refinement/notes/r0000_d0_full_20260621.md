## Result summary

The full-cycle run with immediate loading and immediate drainage is the closest
among the tested early-stage configurations so far. It captures the corrected
Cryer peak and the later dissipation trend reasonably well.

- RMSE over saved post-origin samples: `0.01861`
- MAE over saved post-origin samples: `0.01401`
- Numerical peak: `p^w/p0 = 1.20132` at `T_v = 0.05489`
- Corrected analytical value at the same sample: `1.24151`
- Peak underprediction: `0.04019`
- Final numerical value: `0.00313`
- Final analytical value: `0.00194`
- Final absolute error: `0.00118`

The maximum saved-snapshot velocity is `0.01638 m/s`, and the final saved
velocity falls to `5.21e-05 m/s`. This velocity metric is snapshot-based; with
`tout=0.0025`, the earliest transient may be under-sampled compared with the
shorter `tout=0.001` sweep cases.

## Current conclusion

For the current `dp=0.003` Cryer validation, using no ramp and opening drainage
from the initial time gives the best full-cycle match so far. The remaining
main discrepancy is the early Cryer peak amplitude, which is about `3.2%` low
relative to the corrected analytical peak at the saved peak sample.
