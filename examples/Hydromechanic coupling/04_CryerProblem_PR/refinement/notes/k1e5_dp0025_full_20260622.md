## Two-output strategy

Two runs were combined into one pore-pressure sequence:

- Early dense output: `dp0025_early_tv001_dtv1e-4`
  - `T_v,max=0.01`
  - output interval `Delta T_v=1e-4`
- Full coarse output: `dp0025_full_tv1_dtv1e-2`
  - `T_v,max=1.0`
  - output interval `Delta T_v=1e-2`

The combined sequence contains 199 nonzero-time points.

## Main observations

High-frequency early output captures a strong startup oscillation:

- `T_v=0.0001`: numerical `p/p0=2.7045`, analytical `p/p0=1.0129`
- `T_v=0.0002`: numerical `p/p0=0.4289`, analytical `p/p0=1.0183`
- `T_v=0.0003`: numerical `p/p0=1.8785`, analytical `p/p0=1.0224`

After about `T_v=0.001-0.003`, the center pressure sequence becomes much closer to the analytical trend.

Full combined metrics:

- RMSE over all points: `0.1552`
- MAE over all points: `0.0487`
- Maximum early numerical value: `2.7045` at `T_v=0.0001`
- Maximum speed in early dense output: `0.0761 m/s`

Metrics after excluding the startup interval:

- For `T_v>=0.001`: RMSE `0.0290`, MAE `0.0248`
- For `T_v>=0.003`: RMSE `0.0267`, MAE `0.0233`
- For `T_v>=0.01`: RMSE `0.0208`, MAE `0.0165`

Peak-window comparison:

- Analytical peak: `p/p0=1.2476` at `T_v=0.05`
- Numerical value at `T_v=0.05`: `p/p0=1.2146`
- Peak deficit at the analytical peak time: `-0.0330 p0`

Late-time comparison:

- At `T_v=1`: numerical `p/p0=0.00422`
- At `T_v=1`: analytical `p/p0=0.00178`

## Conclusion

The two-output strategy works: it preserves the dense early-time information without generating excessive files over the full period. The dp=0.0025 full-period result follows the analytical Cryer curve after the very early startup oscillation, and the main peak is correctly phased but about `2.6%` lower than the analytical peak value. The dominant remaining issue is still the initial coupled loading/drainage transient, not the later dissipation trend.
