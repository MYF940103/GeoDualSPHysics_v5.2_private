With xi=Xq−Xi and dx=xq−xi_current, F=(sum w*dx*xi^T)*(sum w*xi*xi^T)^−1. This is an anchored-relative weighted LS fit. F/D2 are finite smoothed patch diagnostics. A cloud crossing a slip/shear discontinuity, rotation, a truncated free boundary or strong heterogeneous deformation need not have a single accurate affine map. Consequently detF need not equal local stage-integrated J/J_PRE, and disagreement is not by itself a J-update defect. Full saved stress is not the unsaved clump-forming force or actual float AS tensor; FS is recomputed at save. Positive saved tension identifies a stress state, not tensile-instability causation.
