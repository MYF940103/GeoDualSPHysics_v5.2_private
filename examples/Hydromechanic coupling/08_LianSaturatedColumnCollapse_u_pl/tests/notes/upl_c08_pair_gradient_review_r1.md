With d=xi-xj, n=d/r and dv=vi-vj, rdot/r=n.dv/r is negative for closing. Local L rows are velocity components and columns spatial derivatives. The double diagnostic sum of the two actual corrected float fluid/bound groups gives D=(L+LT)/2 and W=(L-LT)/2. For mean endpoint gradients, resolved directional strain is n.Dmean.n; the recorded residual is n.[dv-Lmean.d]/r, so resolved+residual equals the actual pair closure algebraically. Negative residual means more closing than the endpoint mean-gradient prediction. It is not a fitted error, hourglass classification or tensile-cause proof: finite separation, nonlinear gradients, unequal/disordered/boundary stencils and history can produce residuals even for smooth motion. No ratio is clipped or used as an acceptance threshold.

The resolved directional strain is split into 2D XZ isotropic area part trace_XZ(Lmean)/2 and XZ deviatoric part. Thus J/area can remain near1 while a material line shortens under shear. This is separate from the 3D constitutive deviator tr(D)/3 used for stress, retaining yy under plane strain. Antisymmetric W has n.W.n=0 analytically and does not change instantaneous pair length; its W.sigma-sigma.W term can rotate the effective stress and affect later mechanics. Raw floating normal-spin values are retained, not set to zero.

Focus CSV preserves the actual nine fluid/bound/sum gradient members, full six-component effective stress and actual Rsigma, theta and primary state. Derived fields compare actual theta with trace(Lsum), XZ symmetric eigenvalues/spin norm, actual full stress-rate norm/trace and a double tensor shadow. Shadow uses actual logged K/G from the sealed Run.out, effective sigma castfloat as in the physical routine, K*trace(D)I+2G*dev3D(D) and W.sigma-sigma.W. It is not literal float replay: production builds D/W per group, uses float stiffness/operation grouping and accumulates each group's stress rate. The reported actual-minus-shadow differences and trace-vs3Ktheta differences are descriptive, not a new numerical pass gate. Spin-commutator trace is analytically zero; pressure is not subtracted from effective sigma for this constitutive-rate decomposition.

The pair CSV also retains actual relative acceleration from both focus force values and the geometric curvature term |v_t|^2/r.
