# r5 .63/.64 pressure pulse: saved-state pressure-channel probe

Author-only `support/upl_c08_r5_pressure_probe_r1.py`; not imported, AST-run or executed. New source/note/output prefix were checked by exists/glob and targeted git ls-files. No solver, BI4 read, decoder, build, old-file edit or SHA scan is part of this tool.

The sole source is the completed root r5 psi0 native300 export. Actual owner run, wrapper/solver exits, postprocess and unique native call must be closed and all original native coverage counts/guards remain required. The explicit memmap profile is300 records,6805 IDs,22 arrays/212B per particle and the original12B header. Every field uses `(6805,components)`, including `(6805,1)` scalars, so the frozen Cloud API consumes the same formal-bundle bytes without rewriting data or adapting N. It does not use the formal plotter's flattened scalar view.

Heavy probes are only actual parts60/61/62/63/64/69 and native soil IDs5809/4956/4959:18 receivers. Their actual tau is read from scalar time minus the certified PRE; part60 is the.61 save, not t=.60. All300 raw saved states of these IDs are retained in a small state CSV/pressure plot, including FS type, native excess versus fixed P0, J/n/rho/k/kappa,6 effective stresses and saved-last-stage theta. A magnified pressure view retains every saved part59..69, including intermediate raw extremes; no points are interpolated or clipped.

The pressure operator is the frozen `upl_c08_pressure_coeff_r1.Cloud`: complete real fluid stencils, V0*savedJ, rebuilt Ci/Cj, original Eta2, LP/Taylor/kgrad coefficients and actual saved FS choice. The pressure-only raw-mechanical channel is exactly `upl_c08_pressure_ace_jac_r1.Mechanical/probe/metrics`, with all self-pressure terms and complete two-hop mechanical/C stencils. Although its historical comment mentions6800, its actual vector length is `cloud.x`; the new explicit input is6805. No old helper or registry is changed.

Original WallStencil checks are mandatory. A receiver less than2R from saved fixed B, any needed C stencil containing a fixed source, or any mechanical stencil containing fixed is recorded as not evaluated; no fixed image is dropped, synthesized or partially truncated to obtain an answer. The selected receivers are expected to be far from the bed, but that expectation never bypasses the check. C uses the helper's exact-nonzero inverse/zero identity policy for fluid correction, not the wall MLS1e-3 criterion.

CSV observations include true FS/non-FS counts within R, nearest other-real-soil and fixed-B distances, current C determinant/condition/components, neighbor C condition and spacing, saved J/kappa/theta, raw/Taylor/kgrad/current pressure rows, complete pressure-to-raw-mechanical-to-Darcy coefficients, pressure-only acceleration and separate divAce/FS-Ace-lx/chain-Ace actions. Units are per-second pressure coefficients and Pa/s channel actions. Negative offdiagonals or nonzero constant1Pa response describe this frozen row; they do not prove a positive global eigenvalue, a fully coupled instability, or the cause of the actual pulse.

All local2R points also receive their complete fluid C moment determinant/condition/eigenvalue or an explicit original-WallStencil exclusion. A separate hypothetical FS-minus-interior row records `Delta lap=(gradp+rho_w*A_pressure_only) dot lx` and the corresponding `Kw/(n*gamma)*ki` rate scale, including its assembled-row roundoff. It is conditional on the same saved geometry and raw pressure-only mechanical acceleration, not actual full Ace, an observed substage switch, or proof that FS caused a pulse.

The mechanical helper includes only the raw pressure force `-rho_i^-1 sum Vj(pi+pj) gradW`. Actual force also has effective stress, AS1 and AV; the pressure-dependent AS spectral derivative and AV/velocity/stress/J/theta evolution are explicitly excluded. Theta is a saved last-stage observation, not a replay of the finished instantaneous dp/dt. Native FS is the saved full-state classification, not proof of the unsaved force/rate substage mask. Packed GPU position/FMA order and half stages are unavailable. This remains a saved-full-state proxy, not exact GPU RHS replay or a complete composite-system causation test.

Fresh outputs are up to7 concise CSVs,3 PNGs and `logs/upl_c08_r5_pressure_probe_r1.check.json`; existing partial or complete products are refused. Local fields show every real soil point within2R, FS2/3 triangle markers and a common signed symlog raw range. Receiver/channel plots also use signed symlog and retain all values. Display thresholds are.1kPa/1Pa/s only, not numerical gates or fit parameters. No equation, dt, threshold, new physical admission, half run or automatic follow-on is changed.
