## Fixed arithmetic checks and interpretation

Reuse the retained direct-sum factor B=32*FLT_EPSILON+4096*DBL_EPSILON before any execution. With s=Kw/n*k/(rho_w*9.80665), budgets are:

- Raw rate: B*max(1,s*sum|w*dp|).
- Corrected rate: B*max(1,s*(sum|w*dp|+sum_components|gp_component*Lx_component|)).
- Affine gradient: B*max(1,sum_j Vj*|dp|*(|gi_x|+|gi_y|+|gi_z|)+|b|).
- LP=b*Lx_x identity: B*max(1,sum|w*dp|+|b*Lx_x|).

These are inherited sourcefloat/direct-sum roundoff scales and their explicit propagation, not newly invented physical accuracy limits. Require da=0, corrected gp=(b,0,0) within the gradient budget, LP=b*Lx_x within its budget, and s*(LP-gp.Lx) within the corrected-rate budget. b=0 must produce a null rate; opposite b fields must reverse actual rates within the sum of their budgets. The actual rate must match the production branch reference within its corresponding budget.

For each kernel, at least one nonzero-b row of predeclared actual flat-top FS ID76 must have both |s*b*Lx_x| and |actual rate| above the raw-rate arithmetic budget.
