# Cached-HG saved-phase review r1

This is a closed, purely offline audit of `c08_h18_hg_cached_window_GPU_r1`, not a solver, native decoder, physics selection or new model candidate. The script consumes the original run/start/call, Release build/source capture and its five frozen private sources, original 91-frame scalar plan, and only `C08CachedPlasticHGPolicy.json` plus the 182-row/60-column `C08CachedPlasticHG.csv`. It does not enter a `native_r1` directory or read BI4/typed particle bodies. Original files remain untouched. The only scientific derivative is `figures/c08_h18_hg_cached_phase_review_r1_summary.csv`; the independent receipt is `logs/c08_h18_hg_cached_phase_review_r1.check.json`. Both use exclusive creation. A failed review preserves its first failure and cannot be automatically repeated.

The owner sealed genuine solver normal completion before its deferred sidecar review. Consequently this audit explicitly labels the sidecar/policy size/mtime as **first review observations**, not historical seals supposedly present in the owner receipt. All already sealed program/build/source/call/plan/input stats are bound before consumption and checked again before the derivative is sealed; no SHA scan is used. The actual original child executable, argv, parent, creation identity and Popen/Win32 exits are compared with run/start, rather than relabelled as this offline tool's process.

The frozen CUDA header is the exact CSV schema authority. Schema2 policy identifies the energy-normalized loading-only HG with a single material producer, a once-consumed cache and original physical CUDA objects. Source locations in `support/c08_h18_hg_cached_r1.cu` are: material Result producer 113–168; raw-input/cache identity and once consumption 181–231; full LS adjoint 346–377; local/global observations 385–445; per-node cast guard 357–376 and exact cache writeback 457–469; phase/consumer/accept lifecycle 544–664; source CSV writer 666–699. The actual build/source capture, rather than an unbound current public source, supplies the consumed source bytes.

Each save contains Pre then Cor. Both share the genuine original source clock n; neither label invents an exported half-time. A scalar `time += 5us` loop starts from the certified source time and checks all 91 saved/source times exactly against the original plan. The final global step is 2180000, with 180000 new updates. At save time `Accept/Counts` updates both recorded phase structs, so both rows must have `pre_calls == cor_calls == commits == completed_step - 2000000`; Pre is not expected to be one call behind. Every exported phase has 5000 produced/consumed/material-checked nodes, 65000 input-scalar checks and 35000 output-scalar checks, valid status totals and directly recorded graph/rank/necessary-dt/local-angle domains. Positive current mass/volume is an internal executed-source guard, not independently reconstructed from this sidecar. The six sigma plus kappa check is a **cache writeback** check from one Result, not an independently repeated original material return.

Pre/Cor `Eold` must have identical double bits, because both start from the same committed directed-row memory. Only the accepted Cor swaps into the next memory. From cold zero memory, `accepted_born - accepted_dead == Cor.rows` is an exact integer identity. Accepted discarded energy and lifecycle counts are monotone observations accumulated from accepted Cor only. Instantaneous Pre/Cor born/dead values need not match. Saved positive work or a nonzero energy is retained; these are not clipped or rejected on a newly invented physical threshold.

The raw source identity is `ledger = Enew - Eold + node_work + loading_loss + Edead`. `Edead` is counted once. `adjoint_defect = node_work - row_work`. Rotation energy is already contained in Enew and hence in the raw ledger; the derivative separately reports `ledger_minus_rotation`, without charging rotation twice. Python `fsum` independently recomputes the scalar energy identity with an explicit Gamma8 floating-addition allowance; this only reconciles the exported numbers. The original CUDA diagnostic budgets are not outward enclosures. Their ratios are reported descriptively, including any exceedances, and are not new physical acceptance gates. `max_commit_error / commit_budget` divides two separately maximized node quantities and therefore is **not** the actual worst node error/budget ratio; the individual node guard remains internal to the executed kernel.

The summary records signed minima/maxima and absolute maxima with actual saved part/time for graph size, condition, necessary-step ratio, distance, angle, material states/gamma/root ratio, energy/work/rotation/ledger, diagnostic ratios and force/cast magnitudes. Units follow the private 2D unit-thickness model: its e-memory energy/work is J/m, force N/m and acceleration m/s²; the interval is the stage width used in the frozen source. The cumulative angle is an envelope formed from per-step maximum local half angles, **not** the net rotation of a named particle.

The 182 rows are sparse save observations of 360000 material/force stages. Normal completion and counters support the source's internal per-stage guards; they do not export all stage values or reconstruct a per-update energy trajectory. Do not sum sparse work/loss or trial Pre and Cor to claim cumulative physical dissipation. Full coupled passivity, physical pressure/clumping/motion benefit, native safety, restart support and physical acceptance remain outside this check. Root independently owns the unique native/physical review.
