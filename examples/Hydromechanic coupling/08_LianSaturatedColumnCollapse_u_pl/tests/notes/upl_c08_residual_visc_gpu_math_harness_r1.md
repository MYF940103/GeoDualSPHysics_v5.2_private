The NumPy reference forms the complete all-soil graph without a neighbor cap, uses the same strict double support and original separately rounded float32 Cubic expressions, eta0-matched LS and beta0 force. It deliberately has no FMA. A support decision within the declared double-roundoff envelope fails rather than changing the graph silently. The original128eps scaled rank criterion remains; no ridge, row removal or fallback is allowed.

Floating budgets are predeclared in the script, not calibrated against a run. Each edge's float-kernel uncertainty is `64*FLT_EPS*scale`, with scale=(|c1 q|+|d1 q^2|)/rad inside h and |c2|(2+q)^2/rad outside; the latter retains an absolute cancellation envelope near the cutoff. This covers the intended fast reciprocal/sqrt/FMA versus separate float32 shadow, and is a conservative arithmetic comparison model, not an external continuum accuracy tolerance. Double sums use gamma_(64*(degree+1)). Absolute moment/normal errors propagate through the unregularized inverse via `||M^-1||/(1-||M^-1||*errorM)`; reaching1 fails. Force and lambda errors propagate those same edge/LS envelopes. Analytic affine/rotation null checks use the correlated N=L M relation, not an assumption that independent weight perturbations destroy affine exactness. DeltaAce, L, lambda and final float addition are reported separately. Final addition also checks the exact computational contract `float64(base)+returned_delta -> float32`; this is not a cross-model0ULP or5Pa physics gate.

Ideal double force and committed float increment each have separate fixed-frame power, net-force and global-torque budgets. Nonpositive ideal mechanical power follows the matched residual projection; float quantization can produce a small bounded change. These checks cannot prove u-pl pressure stability, material accuracy, physical localization or reduced clumping. The per-node lambda already includes alpha once and is only a frozen damping upper bound. This harness does not connect the candidate to production forces/Darcy, does not test a GPU solver OFF path, and does not admit a physical window.
