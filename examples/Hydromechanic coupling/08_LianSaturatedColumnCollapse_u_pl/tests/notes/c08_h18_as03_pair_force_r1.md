The frozen implementation computes AS from the positive spectral part of float(total stress), where total stress is `sigma_eff-p I`, using the original16-iteration float Jacobi routine. It stores `-epsilon*positive(total)` in Pa and later multiplies V_j/rho_i; there is no additional rho^-2 in this u-pl branch. At uniform density/J this has the familiar dry-SPH scaling, but variable-J/mixture inertia and coupled total stress differ from Bui2008's dry density-normalized presentation. Kernel weighting is exactly `[W(r,h)/W(dp,h)]^n`. Bui2008 section4.3 and the cohesive-column discussion recommend epsilon.5/n2.55 for their Cubic h/dp1.2 tests, explicitly warning that n4 can be over-repulsive there; that is not a universal coefficient choice for Wendland h/dp1.8. The current Wendland weight is bounded while its radial gradient tends to zero as r tends to zero: it is not a singular repulsive core. None of these static observations alone identifies the cause of this trajectory's pair collapse or establishes/dismisses a classical kernel-pairing instability.
