## Initial state, inheritance and meaning of pressure

Section4.4 printed18, MD844 says fully saturated soil with a prescribed water table at its upper surface. Printed19, MD887–888 obtains total/effective stress by gravity loading before removing the right wall. The passage does not specify a10s duration, the exact damping schedule, a paper-equivalent geostatic tolerance or a pointwise stored P0 reference. It does not justify resetting pressure or constitutive history on release.

Current retained Def141–148 uses `ConstantZ`, water table z=2m and the certified same-material10s PRE. Original `JSphGpu.cpp:1479–1481,1562–1576` initializes native soil `p=P0=rho_w*abs(g_z)*max(0,z_water-z_initial)`; fixed wall baseline remains affine above the water table. Native p is gauge pore-water pressure relative to atmosphere. P0 is this fixed material reference; it is not recomputed from the current moving surface or changed to the10s preload pressure.

Original `JSphGpuSingle.cpp:1117–1147` first checks source P0 bits against fresh input, then restores source position, velocity/density, all stress components, kappa, p, P0, J/theta and FS state. `JSph.cpp:3807–3815` restores actual source clock/step rather than manufacturing a new t=0 save. Therefore the loader does not intentionally reset collapse pressure to P0. The first receiver save follows a real update and boundary/release operations; comparing its picture with PRE is not a pre-update identity test. Existing qualified native inheritance evidence must be used, not replaced by that picture.

Paper Fig14 discussion printed20, MD891–908 concerns **excess induced by plastic volumetric deformation**, with zero dilatancy giving no such plastic source. This does not require total native p, fixed-material `p-P0`, current hydrostatic excess and a separately attributed plastic excess to be identical. A moving/deforming psi0 material point can change native p through elastic volume change, transport and boundary updates. Comparisons must name the pressure quantity instead of treating any `p-P0` cloud as the paper's exact plotted variable. The supplied text does not give a unique discrete diagnostic decomposition that would permit that identification.

## Decision relevance and limits

The strongest established differences are finite-Kw explicit pressure versus the paper's incompressible TPI/Eq47, FS identification and the unclosed projection reaction. The one-sided pressure inequality and side/bottom slip labels are already directionally consistent. These findings do not establish which difference causes current suction, clumping or early arrest, and do not establish that the paper or current result is wrong.

The previously rejected H1.8 normal-Taylor physical candidate remains rejected: its local affine benefit did not improve the complete pressure/close-pair trajectory (`c08_fs_normal_window_decision_r1.md`). This audit does not reopen it, add a universal p=0 boundary, resume an AS sweep or authorize a TPI/other-psi/long calculation. After the active candidate's independent native/physical review, use these specific model differences to choose one bounded, falsifiable next question under the existing workflow. Full seepage complementarity would need an explicitly defined normal relative flux and its stage/pressure eligibility, not merely smaller negative saved pressure.
