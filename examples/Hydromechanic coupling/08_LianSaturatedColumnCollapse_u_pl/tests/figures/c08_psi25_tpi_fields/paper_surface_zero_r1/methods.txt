Native values are raw particle values at their actual release-relative clocks. No spatial/time smoothing or fit.
Fig. 15: left is the accumulated equivalent plastic strain (0–0.3, 0–1, 0–2 by row). The published inset and arrows remain visible; no reference strain values are inferred from them. Right: excess pressure (−10 to +10 kPa), compared with PorePress minus each particle's fixed PorePress0. The authors describe dilation-induced excess pressure; the exact choice of their reference pressure has not been recovered.
Fig. 16: total PorePress (−10 to +20 kPa); the two printed columns are stabilized and non-stabilized.
Raster-based pressure comparisons sample the nearest original 180 dpi pixel at each native center inside the printed body with a one-pixel eroded mask. The RGB Euclidean distance to the original colorbar must be ≤18 (8-bit RGB). Fig. 15 masks warm ink/annotations together with any indistinguishable warm pixels. Display-range absolute and signed differences compare clipped native pressure with nearest-palette kPa on this common subset; they are descriptive, omit unmatched soil, and do not represent pointwise errors against author-supplied data. Native regional statistics include unmodified out-of-range values. Region x limits use current coordinates; kappa>0.1 defines an active/deformed material subset rather than the exact shear-band boundary. The Fig. 15 negative reference band uses only matched printed cool pixels whose nearest color is below −2.5 kPa.
